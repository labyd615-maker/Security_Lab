## Task 6.2.1 — Reusing IV في OFB

هذا الجزء **نظري** 
Reusing the same key and IV in OFB produces the same keystream.
Knowing one plaintext-ciphertext pair allows the attacker to recover
the keystream and decrypt another ciphertext.
